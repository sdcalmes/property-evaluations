## 2. Buyer-fit review
### Kitchen — 8.5/10 provisional
Visually inspected kitchen photo: light natural wood cabinets, granite counters, hardwood floor, large Wolf range with two oven doors and substantial hood, sink at window, central island, built-in microwave and beverage/coffee area. Substantial prep and appliance capability: this looks useful for someone who actually cooks. It retains a traditional finish palette rather than the current white/black remodel look. Check working aisles when dishwasher/oven doors are open, drawer storage, range age/service records, hood exterior venting, and whether island seating interferes with work.

Pantry is an MLS feature, **not yet a verified walk-in pantry**; floor plan does not clearly establish its size or organization. Pantry score withheld.

### Garage entry / mudroom — 7/10 provisional
Photo floor plan shows direct garage entry into a separate hall, about 12'11 × 6'0, beside laundry and a half bath; kitchen lies beyond. This is useful separation from the living area. Laundry itself is only about 5'8 × 7'11. A hall/closet is not automatically an equipped mudroom: inspect hooks, coat capacity, bench placement and door conflicts. Main-floor fifth bedroom/office is adjacent, about 10' × 13'6 in the plan, differing from MLS 11 × 10.

### Garage / outbuilding — 7.5/10 provisional
Large three-car garage is an immediate positive. The attached garage works even if a future building cannot be approved. No subject-owned shed independently confirmed. **No permission for a 30×40 outbuilding established.** HOA declaration, city zoning, architectural review, setbacks and easements all matter. Do not value the property as if a workshop is assured.

### Basement / bonus room — 9/10 provisional
Unfinished basement and approximately 1,667 sqft excluded basement area on the photo plan make this a strong storage candidate. Bathroom rough-in reported. A large upstairs bonus/office space (plan about 23'1 × 15'1) already provides playroom or office flexibility without consuming basement storage. Tour must check moisture, sump history, radon, foundation and clear ceiling height. Basement finishing remains a future project and is not included in value as completed living area.

### Garden and outdoors — 8/10 provisional
Aerial and deck photographs show broad open lawn with trees mainly around portions of the edges. A sunny 30×40 garden appears physically plausible; **not surveyed or measured**, and fence permission is unknown. Room for play and some fruit trees seems better supported than a large meadow/orchard/workshop program. Verify actual boundaries, easements, full-season sun, drainage and well location before selecting a garden pad.

### Privacy — 6.5/10 provisional
Mature front trees and some side screening are attractive. Rear deck photo clearly shows neighboring houses and a neighbor's pool/fence. Broad lawns connect visually. This is comfortable subdivision space, not secluded woods. Aerials show a natural strip beside part of the site, but ownership and protected status are unverified.

**Weighted buyer-fit approximately 7.4/10**, excluding unverified pantry category. Experimental fit is separate from valuation; restrictions and actual routes may change it.

Basement photo 35 shows open joists, ductwork, posts and exposed concrete slab; slab cracks are visible, with significance unestablished. Some gallery images failed to load; this is not a comprehensive 49-photo or inspection review.

Visible exterior landscaping and paint look orderly; roof appears visually coherent from aerial but age and remaining life cannot be established. Wood siding and multiple roof penetrations/skylights deserve inspection. Kitchen has coherent cabinetry and substantial appliances, rather than just obvious paint-and-hardware staging. Foyer has hardwood and white/wood stair trim; inspected bedroom retains carpet and traditional color. Cosmetic changes are optional preferences, not demonstrated defects.

Deck boards show weathering; no structural conclusion from photo. Inspect ledger flashing, rails, posts and underside. Neighbor visibility reduces deck privacy. Primary bath is marketed with separate shower, jetted tub and dual sinks, but its detailed condition is not independently rated here. Window replacement and glazing age unknown. Nothing established a cheap recent flip, but 26-year ownership does not eliminate deferred capital work.

## 5. Lot / GIS / restrictions
**1.01 acres is listing-reported exclusive lot area**, not a count of neighboring natural land. Parcel survey, plat and easement locations were not obtained. Public GIS layer metadata was located; subject query did not return a usable parcel record. No parcel-specific wetland, floodplain or contour overlay was completed. Therefore no claim of wetland-free land, exact buildable area or permanent undeveloped backdrop.

Aerial suggests gently shaped, substantially mowed yard; elevation/surface drainage still needs an on-site wet-weather review. Do not infer ownership from mowing lines. Adjacent natural vegetation may provide habitat, but title and planning maps must establish whether it is common land, easement, neighbor property or future development ground. The recent Lot 10 transaction means a statement that the subdivision is fully built out would be premature.

Mequon residential architectural guidelines state sheds/accessory structures should match residential usage and compatible materials; metal sheds generally discouraged. This is **design guidance, not evidence a 1,200-sqft outbuilding is allowed**. Obtain a city determination and recorded HOA/ACC rules on building size, fences (especially deer fencing), gardens, orchard planting and setbacks. $350/year is modest financially but says nothing about restriction severity.

## 9. Offer strategy / verdict
- Opening discussion: **$730,000–$740,000** if no competing offer and no inspection red flags.
- Reasonable purchase: **$740,000–$760,000**, contingent on condition/restrictions.
- Caution: **$770,000**.
- Approximate walk-away: **$780,000** on current evidence. This is a buyer discipline boundary, not a known appraisal result.
- At $750,000 with sound inspection, verified dimensions and acceptable rules, this is defensible. Newly listed means little evidence of seller urgency; a low opening may fail, and the Brighton sale demonstrates nearby competition can be meaningful. No proof of current competing offers.
- Restrictions that defeat the garden or workshop can make this a pass even at market value. Conversely, roof/mechanical receipts, a genuinely functional pantry and confirmed permissive covenants strengthen the case.

**What I like:** cooking-oriented kitchen, substantial above-grade layout, bonus room, three-car garage, basement storage, usable lawn. **What holds it back:** uncertain HOA/outbuilding/deer-fence rights, open rear exposure, family access and unknown big-ticket ages. I would tour it for the house and practical yard, withhold the private-acreage premium, and get HOA documents early.
