## 9. Offer and verdict

**Tour candidate with conditions.** The three-car garage, main-floor primary, 1.44-acre recorded lot and relatively moderate Nashotah taxes are appealing. The 3-bedroom count, unverified kitchen/mudroom/garden fit, significant pool obligation and HOA fence/outbuilding rules hold it back. The recent same-street $650k sale is a useful check on the $750k ask.

If a tour confirms good condition, **open around $710,000–$720,000** with a clear pool inspection/credit mechanism. A defensible purchase outcome is roughly **$720,000–$745,000**; **$750,000 is the caution point**, and about **$755,000–$760,000 is a walk-away zone** without unusually strong verified condition and a fully funded liner solution. These are negotiating guideposts, not appraisal certainty. If the seller no longer funds the liner or inspection finds broader pool trouble, subtract the actual independent turnkey cost and risk buffer from those ranges instead of accepting a vague concession. The conclusion could improve with verified excellent interiors, genuinely usable sunny yard and clean pool/mechanical reports.
