## 2. Buyer-fit review

### Kitchen — 7/10; pantry — 6.5/10

Bright and generous eat-in kitchen with substantial perimeter prep counter, tall cabinetry, island seating, sink window and deck access. The island electric range consumes uninterrupted workspace and puts heat near seating; no overhead hood is visible. Verify any downdraft and outdoor discharge. White flat-panel fronts, reddish counters and beige tile read as serviceable older finishes, not demonstrated premium renovation. Check cabinet interiors, drawer quality, clearances and whether the island suits daily cooking. Tall cabinetry offers food storage; a dedicated walk-in pantry is not established.

### Garage entry / mudroom — 5.5/10

Marketing plan suggests a kitchen/eating-area-adjacent garage entry, a garage closet and nearby laundry, rather than a dedicated family drop zone. Laundry photo shows sink/cabinets but little obvious free wall. Test the actual path with shoes, coats, bags and stroller; the front foyer is not evidence of a mudroom.

### Garage / shed / outbuilding — 8/10, provisional

Approximate 23′1″ × 31′7″ plan is promising for three vehicles, but the 3.5-car label is not a workbench-capacity guarantee. No garage interior is pictured. Field-check door widths, columns and clearances with the intended SUV and tools/bikes. Large unfinished basement helps storage. No separate garden shed confirmed. No penalty for lacking a large workshop.

County maps the whole lot A-3. Town §17.04.I generally allows up to two detached accessory buildings totaling 1,000 sf, other accessory buildings up to 400 sf, with 50-foot setback, 25-foot offset and 12-foot accessory height provisions. §17.06 adds compatibility/review rules. These support investigating a small shed, not approval for a specific resin/wood product or location. Covenants, septic/easements and required permits remain unresolved. [Official 2026 Chapter 17, PDF pp. 67–68 and 109](https://www.townofdelafield.org/Documents/Government/Municipal%20Code/FULL%20CHAPTER%2017%20-%202026.pdf?t=202608241326140).

Walkout finished rooms have good daylight. Inspect wall coatings, concealed joints, stored-object areas, sump/drains and humidity; photos do not exclude moisture. Check both fireplaces/chimneys.

Northwest study rectangle is geometric only, not advice to clear trees. Confirm growing-season sunlight, slope/drainage, water access, septic/reserve and fence permission.
