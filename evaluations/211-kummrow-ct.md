# Property Evaluation — 211 Kummrow Ct, Wales, WI 53183

Evaluated October 17, 2026. Buyer-side opinion: **worth touring at $799,900, but not a clear bargain.** Strong kitchen/pantry, mature setting, three-car garage and a useful ranch layout. The outdoor wish list is less certain: this is a landscaped 0.74-acre subdivision parcel, not acreage with proven workshop/garden freedom. Provisional value **$760,000–$805,000**; preferred purchase **$770,000–$790,000** with sound due diligence.

## 2. Buyer-fit and photo review

Directly inspected 16 subject photos: foyer (7), aerial (4), main living/kitchen/dining (8, 9, 10, 12, 14, 17), pantry/adjacent closet (19), lower level (31, 33, 35, 36), lower bath (38), covered patio/deck framing (40, 41). Not every room, front elevation, roof surface, primary bath or garage interior was inspected. Comp interiors were not directly inspected.

**Kitchen — 8.5/10.** Two separate island/prep surfaces, seating, substantial stained cabinetry and direct connection to dining/living space are useful rather than purely decorative. Large windows give excellent light and wooded views. This reads as a well-kept traditional kitchen, not a newly rebuilt luxury kitchen. The small central island could create circulation pinch points with several people cooking. Microwave over range; exterior venting unverified. Confirm refrigerator clearances, drawers, outlet placement and actual counter condition. Listed kitchen is 11×15; dining 9×10, so test real family-table space.

**Pantry — 9/10.** Photo 19 visibly shows a walk-in pantry with open shelving; this is genuine food storage. Adjacent closet has wire shelving, but the photo does not establish its relation to the garage.

**Mudroom — unknown.** Main-floor laundry is listed, yet a useful dedicated garage-entry drop zone has not been demonstrated. Walk the garage-to-kitchen path with coats, boots and bags in mind; do not count the pantry-adjacent closet as a verified mudroom.

**Garage/outbuilding — 7.5/10.** Three-car attached garage plus included storage sheds meet the basic storage preference on paper. Verify depth, door widths, parking plus mower/tool space, heater installation and shed permits. A 30×40 workshop is not established and may conflict with zoning/HOA rules.

**Basement storage — unknown.** Only 1,033 square feet is listed finished beneath a 2,177-square-foot main level. This suggests potential unfinished utility/storage, but subtracting finished footage does not prove usable storage because garage footprint, stairs and foundation layout matter. Obtain floor plan and inspect. Finished rooms are bright for a lower level, with walk-out access, large windows, another fireplace and full bath. Carpet, wood trim and simple bath finishes look serviceable, not luxury renovated. No visible defect in selected photos is not evidence of a dry foundation.

**Outdoor/garden — 5.5/10, provisional.** Aerial and patio views show actual lawn, mature perimeter trees and landscaped beds, so this is more useful than an entirely wooded steep parcel. However the walk-out elevation and boulder/grade transitions show meaningful slope. A continuous sunny, level, fenceable **30×40 garden has not been measured**. Septic tank/absorption/reserve fields and easements may occupy apparent lawn; trees can also shade it. Several fruit trees may be practical; a large orchard plus workshop plus garden would compete with play space.

**Setting/privacy — 8/10.** Cul-de-sac and mature trees are strong positives; a neighbor is visible in the aerial, so privacy is landscaped rather than isolation. Under-deck concrete patio and wraparound deck offer separate outdoor zones. Deck framing needs structural inspection despite a composite walking surface. Cosmetic work appears optional; roof, mechanicals, drainage and septic are separate capital questions.

Buyer-fit score: **7.73/10**, with **82.0% of profile weights rated**; mudroom and unfinished storage remain unrated. This is an incomplete comparison aid, not a valuation formula.

## 4. Lot / GIS / restrictions

Official assessment legal description confirms Lot 7 plus undivided interests in **Outlots 1 and 3**. The 2025 roll reports those outlots as **0.19 and 1.29 acres total**. These are collectively held parcels, not another 1.48 acres exclusively yours and not automatically a conservancy, public trail or protected woods. Exact share, location, permitted use, insurance and maintenance obligations require the recorded plat, declarations and title documents. Outlot 2 is separately village-owned. Do not infer permanent development protection from trees or shared ownership.

Selected aerials support a compact cul-de-sac homesite with lawn and tree boundary. Parcel geometry, frontage, contours, wetland/floodplain intersection, easements, adjoining development proposals and conservation status **not verified**. County Map Atlas and environmental layer resources were located, but no successful parcel-specific overlay analysis is claimed. Mapped wetland would not substitute for delineation.

**Outbuilding caution:** published Wales R-1 and R-2 provisions cap accessory-building floor area at **720 sqft** and accessory height at **15 ft**, with stated setback requirements. A 30×40 structure is 1,200 sqft. Subject district/overlays and code interpretation not yet confirmed, so this is a serious conditional obstacle, not a final parcel-specific zoning determination. Existing sheds do not prove approval for a much larger workshop. Get written Village and HOA answers before assigning workshop value. Garden fencing/tree removal may also require HOA consent.

## 6. Taxes / capital costs

2025 MLS tax **$8,322.66**, about **$694/month** and **1.04% of $799,900**. Official Village 2025 net rate is **$10.198 per $1,000 assessed value** after school credit, before individual credits/charges; lottery maximum $183.05 and first-dollar credit $61.02. $791,500 × 0.010198 ≈ $8,072 before other credits/charges; the mismatch with reported bill is unresolved, likely requiring itemized bill review rather than silently treating either as net check amount. Original bill, fair-market-value field, actual credits and special charges not obtained.

For purchase planning around $770k–$800k, allow roughly **$8k–$9k/year taxes** as an estimate, plus confirmed HOA (~$550/year if current). A purchase does not mechanically reset tax to purchase price; future assessment, levies and charges matter. Well/septic service, insurance and maintenance are extra.

Roof, furnace/AC, water heater, windows, well pump/pressure tank, septic installation/type/service, driveway and deck structural ages are **unknown**. A 2012 build is not proof that all systems remain original or recently replaced. If original, systems are about 14 years old; price inspections and a replacement reserve accordingly. Request roof/insurance history, HVAC labels/service, water-heater label, well test, septic pumping/inspection and permit/design/reserve-field records. Inspect foundation, sump/discharge, radon test/fan, retaining walls, fiber-cement/stone installation and deck ledger/posts/footings. Separate optional paint/lighting/carpet changes from these major items.

## 8. Offer strategy / verdict

**Opening $755k–$765k; reasonable purchase $770k–$790k; caution around $800k; provisional walk-away $805k.** These assume satisfactory inspections, title and restrictions. Lower the ceiling for material defects or poor garden/workshop compatibility. The recent cut invites a disciplined offer, but ~16 days is not a distressed or stale listing. Do not assume an accepted offer exists; current feeds say Active.

I like the actual kitchen function, pantry, light, four bedrooms on the main floor, walk-out lower level, garage and mature setting. I am less convinced it delivers the acreage lifestyle: sunny garden dimensions, workshop approval, common-land protections, mudroom and unfinished storage remain unresolved. **Tour if the kitchen/ranch setting appeals; deprioritize if a 30×40 outbuilding or substantial orchard is essential.** At ~$780k with clean due diligence this is more compelling; near full ask it needs to earn the premium through condition and functional fit.
