## 5. Lot / GIS / restrictions

MLS lists **1.01 exclusive acres**, corner lot, parcel **LSBT0270034**, Arrowhead Highlands. GIS polygon/contours, wetland/flood overlay, exact road setbacks, woods and neighboring development rights **not retrieved**; mapped wetlands, if discovered, would not replace field delineation. Ask for certified survey/plat, recorded subdivision restrictions, deed and Schedule B exceptions, septic plot/soil test and reserve area, grading, any existing easements or outlots.

**Big finding:** Village of Lisbon's **March 12, 2025 zoning code**, Sec. 3(c)(5), provides for under-2-acre residential lots an aggregate **2,000 sq ft accessory-building** floor-area maximum but separate **1,000 sq ft maximum for a qualifying detached garage**, 3%-of-lot accessory-footprint cap, permit requirement regardless of size, compatible design, and setback/offset rules. For recorded subdivisions it prohibits *pole building type construction* except farm operations, requires county environmental health approval for private septic lots, and requires HOA authorization if an HOA applies. A **30×40 = 1,200 sq ft** structure cannot be presumed permissible as detached garage or pole barn. It may have a possible personal-storage classification, **but** recorded covenants, district zoning, 3% area, septic, and frontage setbacks could still prevent it. Obtain written zoning determination for specific intended use/material and footprint.

Sources: [Current Lisbon zoning code](https://www.townoflisbonwi.com/DocumentCenter/View/130/Chapter-11---Zoning-Code---Revised-2025-03-12) PDF pp. 71–75; [Waukesha parcel GIS](https://www.waukeshacounty.gov/parks-and-land-use/land-resources-division/municipal-resources/land-information-systems/). HOA shown blank/$0 on portals **not** verified covenant freedom.
